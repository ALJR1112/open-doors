Mentorship should feel safe.

Open Doors is a student-focused prototype. Real mentor connections would require appropriate screening, privacy protections, reporting tools, and safeguards for minors.

Our safety commitments +
  • Mentor profiles in this demo are fictional examples.
  • Do not share private contact details or sensitive personal information.
  • A real launch should include adult oversight, identity checks, moderation, and clear reporting procedures.
